Bug fixes and improvements

1.0 (21)

11 November 2026

Added

  • Update notifications on Windows and Android — an update button appears when a new build is out; it shows what's new and installs the update in place. On Windows you choose Restart now or When I close the app, and SmarterHumans reopens on the new version by itself.WindowsAndroid
  • Windows installs and updates through Velopack — later updates download only what changed, and the installer and app are signed, so Windows names SmarterHumans as the publisher.Windows
  • Redaction groups — group redactions, and numbering now starts at 1 on every platform.
  • Import and Sync settings in a new design — on every platform.
  • Web import introduction at launch — with "Don't show again".
  • Get started guides for Notes and the Library — short clips in 11 languages.
  • Auto summary setting — turn automatic summaries on or off in Settings → AI → General, and create a summary for any document with "Create summary".
  • AI Settings in the AI Playground — choose the language of generated flashcards, or let the app detect it.
  • Installed version in Settings → What's New on Mac, iPhone and iPad — the same line Windows and Android show.macOSiOS

Improved

  • Runs on older devices — macOS 15, iOS 18, Android 11 and Windows 10 version 1809 (x64) are now supported.
  • Large libraries and typing in Notes are faster
  • Text is easier to read in light and dark mode — placeholders, links, buttons and the selected segment meet WCAG AA contrast.
  • Video and podcast transcripts work without your own Supadata key
  • A note needs a title
  • Tag sorting is consistent — "Created" reverses with the arrow, and each sort word means one thing.
  • Practice settings reworked
  • Self-contained AI flashcard questions — generated questions no longer depend on the text they came from.
  • Notes and concepts from a video stay separate — they are no longer collected into one master note.
  • Older built-in PDF reader removed

Fixed

  • macOS no longer asks for the login-keychain passwordmacOS
  • Dictation no longer types "[BLANK_AUDIO]" — it follows your language, and falls back to Whisper when Apple has no speech assets.
  • Web-imported cards reach your other devices with source, highlights and tags — (IDR-5329, IDR-5328)
  • Web video cards get their transcript marker and Source seek
  • Summaries for imported videos — a video is summarized once its transcript has loaded.
  • Web pages imported from a link render correctly
  • Snip flashcards use your own OpenRouter key when one is set
  • Concepts from a video or podcast no longer titled "page 0"
  • No surprise flashcard generation — returning to a video or podcast with the AI Playground open no longer starts generating by itself.
  • The document is disabled while the AI Playground form is open
  • A restored card is no longer blanked by an empty editor — and **note links break words at punctuation**
